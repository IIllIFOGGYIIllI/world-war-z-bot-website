Privacy Policy

Clear boundaries for community data.

This policy explains what information is handled, why it is used, where it may be processed and how requests can be made. Effective 4 August 2026.

ScopeInformationSourcesPurposesStorageSharingSecurityRetentionYour requestsYounger usersComplaints
Privacy approach: the dashboard uses only the information needed for public status, account verification, community features, security and moderation. The community does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or behavioural analytics trackers.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the World War Z community website, Discord bot, dashboard and connected administration features. The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages, while authenticated operations and persistent bot records are handled by Railway and connected services.

This policy is intended to provide clear information broadly consistent with Australian privacy expectations. Whether a particular statutory privacy requirement applies depends on the circumstances; the community aims to handle information responsibly regardless.

2. Information handled

Public and technical information

  • Public DayZ server state, player totals, capacity, map, platform and update time.
  • Basic request, error, security and operational information generated by the hosting services.
  • Current-tab preferences such as dashboard session state and map view.

Account and community information

  • Discord user identifier, username, display name, avatar, server membership and roles.
  • Linked PlayStation ID and bot-maintained survivor, session, activity, PvP, faction, event and economy records.
  • Support, moderation, warning, note, ban, case, appeal, action and audit information.
  • Evidence or context voluntarily submitted through official community channels.

3. Sources

Information may come from Discord OAuth, Discord guild membership and moderation APIs, user-submitted bot commands, account linking, DayZ or ADM logs, Nitrado server data, dashboard actions, community reports and support tickets.

4. Why information is used

  • Authenticate users and assign visitor, member, Admin or Owner access.
  • Link Discord and PlayStation identities and provide personal bot features.
  • Operate the virtual economy, events, tickets, statistics and server controls.
  • Investigate reports, create moderation cases, enforce bans and detect evasion or abuse.
  • Protect the bot, API, website, database, community and connected accounts.
  • Diagnose failures, maintain audit integrity and improve reliability.
  • Meet platform, security or legal obligations where applicable.

5. Browser storage and cookies

The website does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or behavioural tracking. It uses essential sessionStorage in the current browser tab for the opaque dashboard session and limited interface preferences. This storage is normally removed when the tab closes and is also subject to Railway-side expiry.

Discord, GitHub, Railway, Nitrado or other external services may use their own cookies or storage when you visit or authenticate with them. Their policies apply to that activity.

6. Service providers and disclosure

Information is disclosed only as reasonably necessary to operate or protect the services, including through Discord, Railway, Nitrado, GitHub Pages and authorised community administrators. These providers may process information in countries outside Australia under their own terms and privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect a person from serious harm, to investigate security abuse, or to report conduct to a relevant platform or authority. Personal information is not sold or rented.

7. Security boundaries

  • Discord bot tokens, OAuth secrets and Nitrado credentials remain on Railway and are not included in the public website.
  • The browser receives an opaque session rather than Discord or Nitrado credentials.
  • Railway rechecks current membership and roles before protected requests and actions.
  • Personal endpoints derive identity from the session and cannot select another user's Discord ID.
  • Protected player responses are allowlisted and exclude raw database IDs, Discord IDs, DayZ UIDs, positions, credentials and unrelated records.
  • Write actions require authorisation, target validation, a reason, confirmation, operation-specific safeguards and audit logging.
  • Temporary-ban expiry failures are retried rather than falsely marked successful.

8. Retention

OAuth state expires after approximately 10 minutes, one-time login tickets after approximately 2 minutes, and the dashboard session after approximately 12 hours or when the current tab storage is cleared. Railway stores hashes of temporary login values rather than their usable originals.

Persistent player, economy, moderation, ban, case, support and audit information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the community, enforce restrictions, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, maintain audit integrity, recover accidental unlinking, meet platform requirements or comply with law. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently promised for these operational records.

9. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you through the established Discord support-ticket process. Identity may need to be verified before a request is actioned.

Some information may be retained or withheld where reasonably necessary for security, moderation, ban enforcement, dispute resolution, the rights of others, technical integrity, platform requirements or legal obligations. Requests cannot require the community to alter accurate historical audit records, although a correction or review outcome may be appended.

10. Younger users

Users must meet the age and consent requirements of Discord, PlayStation, DayZ and applicable law. Parents or guardians should supervise accounts where required. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information about a child through public channels.

11. Questions and complaints

Use the established World War Z Discord support-ticket process for privacy questions or requests. If you believe an Australian privacy law applies and a concern is not resolved, information about privacy rights and complaints is available from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
