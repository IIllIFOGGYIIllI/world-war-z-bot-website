Community guidelines

Survive the server. Respect the people.

These standards apply across the connected Discord, bot, dashboard and DayZ community spaces. Effective 4 August 2026.

Respect & safetyFair gameplayAccountsMessages & tradingReportsStaff interactionsEnforcementQuestions
Community standard: play competitively, communicate directly and disagree without targeting people. Server-specific rules posted in Discord form part of these guidelines.

1. Respect and safety

  • No harassment, bullying, stalking, intimidation, credible threats or encouragement of real-world harm.
  • No hateful conduct or targeted abuse based on protected characteristics.
  • No sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, grooming, or content that endangers children.
  • No doxxing, sharing private identifying information, or threatening to expose personal information.
  • No malicious humiliation using another person's real-life image, voice, private messages or personal circumstances.

2. Fair gameplay

  • Follow the current DayZ server rules, safe-zone rules, event rules and raid conditions published by the community.
  • Do not use cheats, unauthorised automation, duplication methods, exploits or knowingly abused glitches.
  • Do not evade bans, restrictions, cooldowns, account links or other enforcement by using alternate accounts or identities.
  • Do not deliberately disrupt events, staff operations or technical systems outside normal permitted gameplay.

3. Accounts, names and identity

  • Do not impersonate community staff, another player, Discord, PlayStation, Nitrado or any official organisation.
  • Do not link a Discord account to a PlayStation ID you do not control.
  • Usernames, profile images and status messages must comply with community and platform rules.
  • Account sharing does not excuse rule violations. The linked account holder may remain responsible for activity through that account.

4. Messages, media and trading

  • No scams, phishing, malicious links, credential requests, malware or fraudulent giveaways.
  • No illegal content or transactions involving prohibited or regulated goods.
  • No sale or purchase of bot currency, server advantages, accounts or community privileges for real money unless the Owner has expressly authorised a lawful official programme.
  • Keep graphic, sexual or disturbing content out of community spaces unless a clearly designated rule permits limited material and all platform requirements are met.
  • Do not spam, mass mention, flood commands or deliberately overload bot and ticket systems.

5. Reports and evidence

  • Use the official ticket or reporting route rather than public arguments or harassment campaigns.
  • Provide complete context where possible. Cropped or edited evidence must not be presented in a misleading way.
  • Do not submit fabricated reports, retaliatory reports or knowingly false accusations.
  • Do not publish sensitive evidence publicly when it can be provided privately to authorised staff.

6. Staff interactions

You may respectfully question a decision or request review. You must not threaten, harass, bribe, impersonate or pressure individual staff members. Staff are also expected to follow the moderation policy, use authorised tools and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.

7. Enforcement

Responses may include guidance, warnings, economy corrections, content removal, Discord kicks, temporary bans, permanent bans, account unlinking or referral to Discord, PlayStation, hosting providers or authorities where appropriate. Context, intent, impact, history, evidence and safety risk may affect the outcome.

See the Moderation & Appeals Policy for cases, evidence, durations and review requests.

8. Questions

Ask through the established Discord support-ticket process. Do not evade a ban or use alternate accounts merely to contact staff.
