Questions

Dashboard help

Why are some buttons still locked?

Discord sign-in now verifies identity, membership and access level. Server-changing and private-data actions remain locked until their own protected endpoints, confirmations and audit logging are completed.

Which displayed information is live?

The DayZ server status, player count, capacity, map, platform and API update time are live. After sign-in, your Discord identity and access level are also live. Balances, tickets, player examples, activity and configuration history remain demonstrations.

What does Discord sign-in request?

The sign-in requests only Discord's basic identify permission. The bot then checks whether that Discord user is currently a World War Z member and calculates member, staff or owner access on Railway.

What comes next?

The next backend stage can connect the verified member session to personal profile and economy information. Protected owner actions will remain separate and require stronger safeguards.
